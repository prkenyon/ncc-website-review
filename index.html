WHO WE ARE

Built on Honesty and Integrity

North Country Construction is locally owned and operated, and we believe in doing business with honesty, integrity, and respect. We treat every home as if it were our own and tailor our services to the needs of each project.

We specialize in custom homes, remodels, and additions, while offering the experience and expertise to tackle a wide range of other construction and home improvement projects. We take pride in the quality of our workmanship and our commitment to delivering outstanding results.

Our goal is to build lasting relationships with our clients through clear communication, dependable service, and quality work. Your trust matters to us, and we strive to make every project one you can be proud of.
